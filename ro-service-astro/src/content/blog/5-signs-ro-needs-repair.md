Your RO water purifier is designed to be reliable. But when something goes wrong, it doesn't just stop working — it gives you warning signs first.

**Catching these signs early can save you from an expensive repair or, worse, drinking unsafe water.**

Here are the 5 most critical warning signs every RO owner should know.

## 1. Water Tastes Strange or Smells Bad

If your RO water suddenly tastes metallic, bitter, or smells musty — **stop drinking it immediately.**

**What it means:**
- Carbon filter is exhausted
- Bacteria buildup in the tank
- Membrane failure allowing contaminants through

**What to do:** Book a service within 24 hours. Meanwhile, use boiled or bottled water.

## 2. Water Flow Has Slowed Down Drastically

Your RO used to fill a glass in 10 seconds. Now it takes 30. That's not normal.

**What it means:**
- Sediment filter is completely clogged
- Inlet water pressure is too low
- Membrane is partially blocked

**What to do:** Don't force it — reduced flow strains the pump. Book a service.

## 3. TDS Reading Is Above 150 ppm

If you have a TDS meter, check the output water. Healthy RO water should be **below 100 ppm**. Above 150 ppm, something is wrong.

**What it means:**
- RO membrane is failing
- Bypass valve is stuck open
- Post-carbon filter needs replacement

**What to do:** Get a professional TDS test done and membrane checked.

## 4. Water Is Leaking from the Purifier

Even a small drip can cause big damage over time — electrical shorts, mold growth, water damage.

**What it means:**
- Worn O-rings or seals
- Cracked housing
- Loose pipe connections
- Damaged storage tank

**What to do:** Turn off the inlet valve, unplug the unit, and call a technician. Don't try DIY fixes on electrical components.

## 5. Motor Is Making Loud or Unusual Noise

A healthy RO pump makes a soft humming sound. If you hear grinding, screeching, or clicking — that's a problem.

**What it means:**
- Pump bearing failure
- Air trapped in the system
- Motor is about to fail

**What to do:** **Turn off the unit immediately.** Running a failing pump can cause electrical damage or flooding.

## When to Repair vs Replace?

**Repair if:**
- Purifier is less than 5 years old
- Only one component is failing
- Repair cost is under 40% of new unit price

**Replace if:**
- Purifier is 8+ years old
- Multiple components failing
- New technology (like copper + RO) offers better value
