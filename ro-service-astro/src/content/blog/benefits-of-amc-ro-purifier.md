Every RO owner faces the same question: **Should I take an AMC or pay for service as needed?**

The math is simple — and often surprising. Let's break it down.

## What Is AMC?

**AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract)** is a yearly plan where you pay a fixed amount upfront and get:

- 3-4 scheduled service visits per year
- Free filter replacements (as needed)
- Priority customer support
- Discounts on spare parts (10-20%)
- Free diagnosis and minor repairs

## 7 Reasons AMC Is Worth It

### 1. Fixed Cost, No Surprises
You know exactly what you'll pay for the year. No sudden ₹2,000 repair bills.

### 2. Free Filter Replacements
Filters cost ₹400-1,500 each. AMC often includes 2-4 filter replacements per year.

### 3. Priority Service
AMC customers get same-day or next-day service. Non-AMC might wait 2-3 days.

### 4. Longer Purifier Life
Regular servicing extends your purifier's life by 3-5 years. Saves ₹10,000+ on replacement.

### 5. Better Water Quality
Scheduled maintenance = consistent water quality. No "taste changes" between services.

### 6. Emergency Support
Some AMC plans include 24/7 emergency support — critical if you have kids or elderly parents.

### 7. Warranty Protection
Regular servicing maintains your manufacturer's warranty. Skipping service can void it.

## What's Typically Covered?

### Usually Included:
- Filter replacements (sediment, carbon)
- General cleaning
- Water quality testing
- Minor repairs
- Labour charges

### Usually NOT Included:
- RO membrane replacement (₹1,200-2,000)
- Motor replacement (₹1,500-3,000)
- Tank replacement (₹1,500-2,500)
- Physical damage

**Always read the AMC terms carefully before signing up.**

## Who Should Take AMC?

**Definitely take AMC if:**
- You use the purifier daily (family of 3+)
- Water TDS is above 300 ppm
- You live in an area with hard water
- Purifier cost over ₹10,000
- You don't want to think about maintenance

**Skip AMC if:**
- You use the purifier occasionally (vacation home)
- Purifier is over 8 years old (replacement better)
- Water TDS is very low (below 150 ppm)

## How to Choose the Right AMC Provider

**Look for:**

1. **Service network** — Can they reach you same day?
2. **Genuine parts** — Do they use authorized spares?
3. **Reviews** — What do Google reviews say?
4. **Transparent pricing** — Hidden charges?
5. **Warranty** — Do they guarantee their work?

**Red flags:**
- AMC under ₹1,500 (quality compromise)
- No written contract
- Non-refundable advance
- Vague coverage terms
