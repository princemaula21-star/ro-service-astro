Your RO water purifier is silently working 24/7 to give your family safe drinking water. But like any machine, it needs regular servicing to keep performing at its best. The question is — **how often?**

The short answer: **Every 4-6 months for a general service, and once a year for a full overhaul.**

But there's more to it. Let's break it down properly.

## The Ideal RO Servicing Schedule

### Every Month (DIY Check)
- Check for water leakage around the purifier
- Listen for unusual motor sounds
- Taste the water — if it tastes odd, something's wrong
- Check the TDS meter reading (should be below 100 ppm)

### Every 4-6 Months (Professional Service)
- Complete filter cleaning
- Sediment filter check
- Water flow test
- TDS level test
- General health check

### Every 12-18 Months (Full Maintenance)
- Sediment filter replacement
- Carbon filter replacement
- RO membrane inspection
- UV lamp check (if applicable)

### Every 24-36 Months
- RO membrane replacement (if needed)
- Pump inspection
- Complete system overhaul

## Why Bangalore Water Needs Special Attention

Bangalore's water supply is notoriously hard. Depending on your area:

- **Whitefield, Marathahalli** — High TDS (300-600 ppm), more sediment
- **Koramangala, HSR** — Moderate TDS (200-400 ppm)
- **Electronic City, Sarjapur** — Variable quality depending on source
- **Old Bangalore (Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar)** — Older pipelines, more rust

Higher TDS means **faster filter wear and tear**. If you live in an area with 500+ ppm TDS, your filters will need replacement sooner than the manufacturer suggests.

## Signs You Need Service NOW (Not Later)

Don't wait for the 4-6 month mark if you notice:

1. **Slow water flow** — Clogged sediment filter
2. **Bad taste or smell** — Carbon filter exhausted
3. **TDS levels rising** — Membrane issue
4. **Frequent motor running** — Pressure problem
5. **Water leakage** — Seal or O-ring issue
6. **Yellowish water** — Pre-filter failure
