TDS — Total Dissolved Solids — is a measurement of all the dissolved minerals, salts, and metals in your water.

**In Bangalore, TDS levels are often dangerously high (300-800 ppm).** For healthy drinking water, TDS should be **below 300 ppm** (ideally below 150 ppm).

Here's how to fix it.

## What Is Safe TDS for Drinking Water?

| TDS Range | Quality |
|---|---|
| 0-50 ppm | Very pure (some minerals stripped) |
| 50-150 ppm | **Ideal** — Pure + minerals retained |
| 150-300 ppm | Acceptable for drinking |
| 300-500 ppm | High — not ideal, RO recommended |
| 500-1000 ppm | Very high — health risk |
| 1000+ ppm | Unsafe — do not drink |

**WHO recommends:** TDS below 500 ppm for drinking water. For optimal health: 150-300 ppm.

## 7 Ways to Reduce TDS at Home

### 1. Install an RO Purifier (Most Effective)
RO removes **up to 95-99% of TDS**. If your water is at 800 ppm, RO output will be around 20-40 ppm.

**Best for:** High TDS areas (above 500 ppm)

### 2. Use TDS Controller
Modern RO purifiers have a TDS controller that lets you set the desired TDS level. This preserves essential minerals while filtering contaminants.

**Best for:** Adjustable TDS output

### 3. Boiling (Partial)
Boiling kills bacteria but **does not remove TDS**. In fact, it slightly increases TDS because water evaporates.

**Best for:** Bacteria removal only, not TDS

### 4. Water Softeners
Softeners use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium (hardness). They reduce TDS partially.

**Best for:** Hard water with high calcium

### 5. Distillation
Distillation removes almost all TDS (0-5 ppm). But it's slow, energy-intensive, and removes essential minerals.

**Best for:** Industrial use, not homes

### 6. Activated Carbon + Sediment Filters
These remove some suspended solids and chemicals. But they don't remove dissolved salts.

**Best for:** Pre-treatment, not main treatment

### 7. UV + UF Purifiers
Kill bacteria and remove particles, but leave TDS unchanged.

**Best for:** Low TDS water needing biological safety

## The Best Solution: RO + TDS Controller

**Combining RO with a TDS controller** gives you the best of both worlds:
- Removes 95%+ of contaminants
- Retains essential minerals (calcium, magnesium, potassium)
- Gives you control over final TDS

Most modern RO purifiers (Kent, Aquaguard, AO Smith) include this feature.

## What If TDS Is Too Low?

Water with TDS below 50 ppm can taste flat and lacks essential minerals. Long-term consumption of ultra-low TDS water can lead to mineral deficiencies.

**Solution:** Use a TDS controller or add a mineral cartridge.

## Signs Your Water Has High TDS

- **Taste:** Salty, bitter, or metallic
- **Appearance:** Cloudy or discolored
- **Deposits:** White scale on taps and utensils
- **Soap:** Doesn't lather well
- **Health:** Digestive issues, kidney stress (long-term)
