You walk into the kitchen and there's a puddle of water under your RO purifier. What now?

**First, don't panic.** 80% of RO leaks are simple issues that can be fixed quickly. But you need to identify the cause first.

Here are the 7 most common causes — and what to do about each.

## Immediate First Steps

Before investigating:

1. **Turn off the inlet water valve** (usually a small knob on the wall)
2. **Unplug the purifier** from the electrical socket
3. **Wipe the area** and place a towel to prevent water damage
4. **Wait 10 minutes** for the system to settle

Now you can safely investigate.

## The 7 Common Causes

### 1. Loose Pipe Connections
**Most common cause.** The inlet or outlet pipes can loosen over time due to water pressure.

**Signs:** Leak is at the pipe junction, not the purifier body

**Fix:** Hand-tighten the connection (don't over-tighten). If still leaking, replace the connector.

### 2. Worn O-Rings or Seals
O-rings and seals wear out every 12-18 months.

**Signs:** Small drip around the filter housing or membrane housing

**Fix:** Replace the O-rings. This is a ₹100-200 part.

### 3. Clogged or Damaged Sediment Filter
A clogged filter increases pressure, causing leaks.

**Signs:** Slow flow + leak, filter looks brown/black

**Fix:** Replace the filter. ₹399-599.

### 4. Cracked Storage Tank
Over time, plastic tanks develop micro-cracks.

**Signs:** Leak is at the tank base, water pools under the tank

**Fix:** Tank replacement (₹1,500-2,500) or new purifier if old.

### 5. Faulty Float Valve
The float valve controls water level in the tank. If stuck, it causes overflow.

**Signs:** Tank keeps overflowing even when full

**Fix:** Clean or replace the valve. ₹300-500.

### 6. High Inlet Water Pressure
If your building has very high water pressure, it can blow seals.

**Signs:** Leak starts immediately after tap opened

**Fix:** Install a pressure reducer. ₹500-1,000.

### 7. Damaged Housing
The filter housing can crack due to age or physical damage.

**Signs:** Visible crack in the housing

**Fix:** Housing replacement. ₹800-1,500.

## DIY vs Professional — When to Call for Help?

**Try DIY if:**
- Loose pipe connection
- Simple O-ring replacement
- Filter replacement

**Call a professional if:**
- Leak is inside the purifier body
- Electrical components involved
- Tank or housing crack
- Multiple leaks at once
- Repair cost over ₹1,500

**Never open electrical components yourself.** Risk of shock or electrocution.

## Prevention Tips

1. **Service every 4-6 months** — catches issues early
2. **Check for leaks monthly** — quick visual inspection
3. **Replace filters on time** — prevents pressure buildup
4. **Install pressure regulator** if building has high pressure
5. **Don't over-tighten pipes** — can crack housing
