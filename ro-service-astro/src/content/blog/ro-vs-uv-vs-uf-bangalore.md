If you're shopping for a water purifier in Bangalore, you've probably seen three terms: **RO, UV, and UF.**

Each technology works differently. And with Bangalore's varying water quality, choosing the wrong one wastes money and doesn't give you safe water.

Let's settle this once and for all.

## The 3 Technologies Explained Simply

### RO (Reverse Osmosis)
**How it works:** Forces water through a semi-permeable membrane that blocks dissolved solids, heavy metals, salts, and contaminants.

**Removes:** Up to 95-99% of TDS, bacteria, viruses, heavy metals, chemicals

**Best for:** High TDS water (above 200 ppm)

### UV (Ultraviolet)
**How it works:** Uses UV light to kill bacteria and viruses. Doesn't remove dissolved solids.

**Removes:** Bacteria, viruses, pathogens

**Best for:** Low TDS water (below 200 ppm) that's biologically unsafe

### UF (Ultrafiltration)
**How it works:** Uses a fine membrane to filter out particles, bacteria, and some viruses. Doesn't remove dissolved salts.

**Removes:** Suspended particles, bacteria, some viruses

**Best for:** Municipal water with low TDS and moderate contamination

## Which One for Bangalore?

**The honest answer: For most of Bangalore — RO is the only safe choice.**

Why? Because Bangalore's water supply has:

- **High TDS** (200-800 ppm depending on area)
- **Hard water** (high calcium and magnesium)
- **Dissolved salts and metals** from aging pipes
- **Variable quality** from source to source

UV and UF **do not remove TDS**. If your water has 500 ppm TDS, a UV purifier will kill bacteria but the water will still have high salt content.

## What About Taste?

RO water is **mineral-free** by nature. Some people find it "flat" tasting. Modern RO purifiers add back essential minerals like calcium and magnesium.

If you care about taste:
- Choose RO + Mineral Booster
- Or RO + Alkaline technology
