Bangalore's water quality in 2026 has changed. Monsoon patterns shifted, new borewells dug, and BWSSB supply expanded. Here's the latest data.

## Bangalore Water Quality Overview (2026)

**Average TDS:** 380 ppm (up from 340 in 2024)
**Average Hardness:** 280 mg/L (very hard)
**pH:** 7.2-7.8 (slightly alkaline)
**Chlorine:** 0.5-1.5 ppm (safe range)

## Why Water Quality Changed in 2026

1. **Monsoon deficit** — 2 consecutive weak monsoons
2. **Groundwater depletion** — Borewells deeper, more minerals
3. **Aging pipelines** — 40+ year old pipes in old areas
4. **Cauvery expansion** — New areas covered, quality varies
5. **Industrial growth** — More pollution risk in certain zones

## Seasonal Variation

| Season | TDS Change |
|---|---|
| Monsoon (Jun-Sep) | -15% (dilution) |
| Winter (Nov-Feb) | +5% (stable) |
| Summer (Mar-May) | +25% (concentration) |
| Post-monsoon | +10% (residue) |

**Test your water 2-3 times a year** — especially before and after monsoon.

## Health Concerns 2026

**Bangalore water now contains:**
- Higher fluoride in some areas (0.8-1.5 ppm)
- Traces of heavy metals in industrial zones
- Bacterial contamination in old pipelines
- Pharmaceuticals (trace levels)

**RO purification removes 95%+ of these contaminants.**
