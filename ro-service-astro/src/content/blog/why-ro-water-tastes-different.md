"I don't like the taste of RO water."

We hear this from customers every day. But here's the truth: **RO water doesn't have a "bad" taste — it just tastes different from what you're used to.**

Here's why, and how to fix it.

## What RO Water Actually Tastes Like

RO water is **pure H₂O** with 95%+ of dissolved solids removed.

**Characteristics:**
- Neutral taste (no sweetness, saltiness, or bitterness)
- Very clean finish
- Slightly "flat" or "empty" for some people
- Cooler sensation (higher heat conductivity)

## Why Some People Find It "Flat"

### 1. Missing Minerals
Normal water has trace minerals — calcium, magnesium, potassium. These add a subtle taste. RO removes them.

### 2. Zero Sodium
Regular water has small amounts of sodium (salt). RO removes 95%+. The absence of salt = "flat."

### 3. Higher Dissolved Oxygen
RO water often has more dissolved oxygen, giving it a slightly different mouthfeel.

### 4. Cold Temperature Sensation
Pure water feels colder on the tongue. This alters perception.

### 5. Psychological
If you're used to tap water taste, RO water seems "bland" initially.

## It's Actually Better For You

RO water isn't just safe — it's better:

- **No heavy metals** — Lead, arsenic, mercury removed
- **No pesticides** — Removed by RO membrane
- **No bacteria/viruses** — Removed by UV + RO
- **No microplastics** — New RO membranes filter these
- **Consistent quality** — Every glass tastes the same

**Yes, some minerals are removed — but these are easily replaced through food.**

If your RO water **suddenly** tastes different:

| Change | Cause |
|---|---|
